#+TITLE: Nazca Plans
#+AUTHOR: Daniel Okuniewicz Jr.

* Plans
Design notes, one self-contained plan per file, ordered by dependency.

- [[file:rendering.org][rendering.org]] -- the rendering pipeline: input,
  tile grids, prepare, tile cache, raster, styling, hit-testing, camera,
  backends, globe, v1 features and the v1 slice (2026-10-29).
- [[file:gl-context.org][gl-context.org]] -- the GL context: taking =glow=
  out of the =gl= API, and what removing it outright would take
  (2026-11-10, proposal).
- [[file:state-picking.org][state-picking.org]] -- picking and SVG through
  feature state, so a layer drawn only through a state picks and exports what
  is on screen (2026-11-16, built in 0.2.1).
- [[file:custom-symbols.org][custom-symbols.org]] -- markers, dashed lines
  and pattern fills from embedder-supplied symbols, on screen, in picks and
  in SVG (2026-11-19, proposal).

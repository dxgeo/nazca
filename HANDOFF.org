** WHERE IT STANDS
Custom symbols' steps 0 to 4 are built and committed on =custom-symbols=,
unreleased:

- *Step 0*: nazca's own =gl::Context=.
- *Step 1*: symbols, the atlas and the 256 x 7 palette.
- *Step 2*: markers, on screen, in picks and in SVG from pixels. Their SVG
  markup moved to step 5 (§ OPEN QUESTIONS 8).
- *Step 3*: dashes, and a border two polygons share drawn once, by the
  last feature fed that outlines it (§ OPEN QUESTIONS 9). Checked natively
  and in Chrome's WebGL2 by the spike's dash check (=--check-dashes=, X in
  the browser).
- *Step 3's two additions*, built 2026-11-23 and checked natively and in
  Chrome's WebGL2:
  - /A far line start keeps its phase/: =Mesh::distances= are =f64=, and
    the upload gives each run of chained segments a shared =f32= base and
    each segment its offset (=SEGMENT_BYTES= 40). No jitter between
    vertices at 1,000 or 5,000 km along; the run is off by one error, up
    to about a pixel at zoom 20.4, a jump where it meets the next run.
  - /=MeshBuilder::line_length=/ measures a piece as nazca does, so the
    next piece's start joins exactly: 0.0 pixels at every level in the
    dash check, measured by a different tile at the level than the seam's.

- *Step 4*: pattern fills, built 2026-11-24. The repeat comes from the
  fragment's pixel and the screen centre's place in each class's repeat,
  reduced in =f64= on the CPU per draw, not from tile coordinates
  (§ Pattern fills says why). =update_palette= now takes
  =&mut GpuPalette=. Checked by the spike's pattern check
  (=--check-patterns=, T in the browser) at z2.3, z14.3 and z20.3, and P
  or =--patterns= puts a hatch or a stipple over the counties. Views
  without patterns are byte-identical to step 3's. The same check passes
  in Chrome's WebGL2.

§ CHECKS (/Dashes/, /A far line start/, /Patterns/) has the runs and
numbers.

** ALSO OPEN
- *Darker dots at a translucent line's vertices.* Each segment's round end
  overlaps the next one's, so the line blends twice there. This is a known
  limit, in plans/rendering.org § BACKENDS, from before this branch and
  separate from step 3.
- After step 5: step 6 and the 0.3.0 release, whose notes cover the
  =update_palette= change with the two breaks.

** RUNNING THINGS
- Native: =cargo run --release --features gl --example spike=. Add
  =-- --outlines dashed= to start dashed. D cycles the outlines, P the
  patterns, H hovers the county picked last, and =--check-dashes= and
  =--check-patterns= run their checks and exit.
- Browser: =cd spike-web && wasm-pack build --target web --release=, then
  =python3 -m http.server= there and open the page. X runs the dash check
  and T the pattern check, each logging its report to the console.
- Tests: =cargo test --all-features=.

** NEXT SESSION: TWO ADDITIONS TO STEP 3, BEFORE STEP 4
Both decided 2026-11-23 (user decision), from measurements the dash check
reports. The plan's § CHECKS records them under /A far line start/ and
/Dashes/.

*** 1. Cure the far line start's jitter
A line's distances are stored as =f32= in local units and multiplied by the
pixels per unit in =f32= in =LINE_VS=, so the dash phase loses precision far
along a line. Measured at z20, as the shader multiplies:

| Along the line | Phase off by up to | Between neighbouring vertices |
|----------------+--------------------+-------------------------------|
| 1,000 km       | 0.50 px            | 0.84 px                       |
| 5,000 km       | 2.00 px            | 3.16 px                       |

Two =f32= for the distance won't cure it: the product with the scale loses
as much again. The cure the plan names is a base that a run of segments
shares, plus each segment's offset from it (=SEGMENT_BYTES= 36 to 40). The
run then carries one error, a jump at its seam, with no jitter between its
vertices. To decide when building it: where the base lives in =Mesh= (a run
per line or ring, or per clipped run), and how the shader reduces the
base's product modulo the pattern before adding the offset's. Rerun
=--check-dashes= and compare the jitter it reports with the table above.

*** 2. A function that measures a line as nazca does
=MeshBuilder::set_line_start= takes where a piece of a longer line starts.
A start the embedder measures along its own geometry misses nazca's
measure, which runs along the line as snapped and decimated on the tile
level's grid. A 20 km line wiggling 5 m every 10 m missed by 12 to 181 px at
z8 to z18, both ways: snapping drops some wiggles and lengthens others. The
fixture's simplified coastlines miss by under 1 px.

The grid is fixed in map units for a level, the same for every tile, so a
function can measure a line exactly as any tile at that level would. An
embedder that measures each piece with it gets starts whose seams join
exactly. Things to settle when adding it:

- its home and signature (on =TileGrid= or =Detail=, taking the level, or
  on =MeshBuilder=);
- that it snaps and decimates as =MeshBuilder::points= does, from the
  piece's own start;
- a unit test that a line cut into pieces, the second started from the
  measured length of the first, gives the same distances as the line whole;
- the dash check's seam line rerun with measured starts, expecting jumps of
  0, next to the source-measured ones;
- the =set_line_start= docs and the plan's § Dashes pointing at it.

** RUNNING THINGS
- Native: =cargo run --release --features gl --example spike=. Add
  =-- --outlines dashed= to start dashed. D cycles the outlines, H hovers
  the county picked last, and =--check-dashes= runs the check and exits.
- Browser: =cd spike-web && wasm-pack build --target web --release=, then
  =python3 -m http.server= there and open the page. X runs the dash check
  and logs its report to the console.
- Tests: =cargo test --all-features=.
